WHAT IS IT?

What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as servers, storage, databases, networking and software through the internet.

Instead of buying and maintaining expensive hardware, organizations can use cloud resources whenever they need them and pay according to their usage.
